Mirrors

Mirror a server New mirror

A mirror copies a bucket on another S3 service into a bucket here, on a schedule, while the bucket stays read only. When you're ready to move, stop writes to the source and freeze the mirror: a final pass copies the last changes and checks the two buckets match. Then complete it to open the bucket to its users. Mirror a server lists every bucket on another service and makes a mirror for each one you pick.
